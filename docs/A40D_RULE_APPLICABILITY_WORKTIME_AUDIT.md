# A4.0D rule applicability + worktime audit

## Source basis

Dok. 25 2026–28 distinguishes ordinary service travel in chapter 18 from holiday stays with patients/clients in chapter 20.

### Point 20.1 — applicability

Chapter 20 applies to holiday stays in Norway or abroad with patients/clients and explicitly states that the rules do not apply to day trips. FERIETUR01 therefore does not finalize a same-day trip using the chapter-20 model.

### Point 18.4 — travel time

Travel time during ordinary working time counts fully as working time. Travel time outside ordinary working time is compensated with ordinary hourly pay, with a separate rule when the journey was not known by the previous day.

For the normal-roster comparison model, the stored ground roster is the app's available evidence of ordinary working time. Travel without supervision responsibility is therefore included in worktime control only for the portion that intersects that roster. Payment remains governed by the A4.0 travel calculation.

## Product boundary

This slice does not add a general day-trip/service-travel calculator. It prevents the holiday-stay calculator from applying chapter 20 to a case that chapter 20 excludes. A future day-trip mode would need its own chapter-18/ordinary-overtime product contract.

## Payment scenario

`Ikke avklart ennå` is an explicit user choice and is treated as a valid documentation state. It does not change arithmetic or determine legal payment liability.
