# A59 — Step 6 calculation result hierarchy

Step 6 is rebuilt as a result overview rather than an inline audit report.

Main screen:
- prominent total
- compact calculation rows
- separate "Allerede dekket av grunnturnusen"
- Day-for-day control as a drill-down
- Rules and calculation basis as a drill-down

Each calculation row opens a Material bottom sheet containing the full existing
explanation, evidence and source. Day audit first shows a compact list of dates;
a date opens that day's full contributions. Covered-roster evidence and hourly
rate/rules are also moved to dedicated sheets.

No calculation, tariff, payment-treatment, daily-audit, roster, settlement, PDF
or flow formula is changed.
