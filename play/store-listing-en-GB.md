## App name

Ferietur

## Short description (max 80 characters)

Plan staffed holiday trips and build an explainable pay basis. Norwegian-language app.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Ferietur is an independent planning and calculation tool for holiday stays where staff accompany
the person travelling. The app is in Norwegian and is built around the Oslo kommune collective
agreement (Dok. 25, 2026–2028).

The app helps structure the trip, working hours, roster, travel and the relevant supplements, and
can produce a PDF with an explainable calculation basis. Trips and drafts are stored locally on
the phone.

**Important:** Ferietur is not an official app from Oslo kommune and does not represent Oslo
kommune or any other public authority. The calculations are indicative and must always be checked
against the applicable collective agreement, roster, agreements and pay information before they
are used as a basis for payment or decisions.

### Official sources

Parts of the rules and pay tables in Ferietur are based on public information from Oslo kommune:

- Oslo kommune — Tariffoppgjør 2026: https://www.oslo.kommune.no/jobb-i-oslo-kommune/tariffoppgjor/
- Oslo kommune — Jobb i Oslo kommune / pay table from 1 May 2026: https://www.oslo.kommune.no/jobb-i-oslo-kommune/

The app also shows which rule and pay-table version was used, both on screen and in the PDF.

### Privacy

Ferietur requires no account, has no ads or analytics and has no internet access. Everything you
enter stays on the device. PDFs are shared only when you choose to share them.

Privacy policy: https://brealorg.github.io/ferietur/privacy/
